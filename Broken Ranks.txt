==================== Broken Ranks: Instance Clear Automation ================
The entire process is structured as follows:

1) Entering the boss instance.

2) Defeating 4 groups of enemies along a fixed path:
a) Each encounter features different enemy types with varying DPS and health pools.
b) Spell hit chances and damage output for both the player and enemies depend on the level difference.
c) If the character is significantly overpowered, automating this specific process becomes less optimal (time-wise).
d) Dying results in teleportation back to the instance entrance.
3) Engaging the final boss after clearing all preliminary groups.
4) Exiting the instance, which teleports the character back to the starting map.

Execution and Performance:
To automate this, I utilized TinyTask to record the full instance clear, resulting in a loop duration of approximately 12 minutes. This was implemented before I transitioned to Python—the main challenge was automating 5 consecutive turn-based fights with enemies moving across a 2.5D map using only a recorded macro. For comparison, a manual session takes about 7 minutes.

System Recommendations:

- High-tier equipment.
- A character level slightly higher than the enemies to ensure faster and safer clears.
- Utilizing Area of Effect (AoE) spells at the beginning of each round to maximize damage output.

Troubleshooting and Edge Cases:
What happens if the character dies?
With the recommended setup, the probability of dying is effectively zero. If death occurs, the loop breaks, highlighting the importance of the prerequisites.

Is enemy movement on the map an issue?
Not at all. By designing the loop with a broad click pattern, it successfully covers all possible enemy spawn locations and movements.

Couldn't this be done with Python?
It could, but this project was a proof-of-concept for macro-based UI automation. Developing a complex Python script for this specific boss was not time-efficient regarding the expected return on investment (ROI).

How stable was the loop?
During testing, I stopped it manually after 5 hours of continuous, successful operation.

Alternative Strategies:
There are more efficient in-game monetization methods, such as automating flask production. However, that process requires significant storage capacity and a high enough character level to make low-level enemies neutral (non-aggressive). While superior in yield, it has a much higher barrier to entry (initial capital and level constraints).
