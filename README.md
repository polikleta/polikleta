Scripts written in Excel with many tables for many possibilities of earning silver which I used and calculated.

* Profit from islands on t6-t7 fishermen (price of upgrading and preparations)
* Profit from chest crafting (simple one)
* Rune/soul upgrading items 4.0 and 5.0 to 4.1/4.2 and 5.1/5.2

* Table of profit (America server only) for journals transport from Caerleon to Lymhurst (special method)
* Table of taxes when buying/selling items with examples
* Big table of buying maps from Brecilien and reselling to other cities
* Table of dividing profit between party members, where some dont get 100%
Extra 2 sheets with calculations made by hand and less important things
